# Chapter 27. Percolation and the Curve y^2 = x^3 + 1

Can one random variable join Langlands' critical percolation work to his reciprocity programme? Cardy's formula is the Beta(1/3, 1/3) distribution function evaluated at the modular lambda function, and the log odds of that law is an extended generalized gamma convolution (GGC) with Thorin atoms at n + 1/3. The crossing threshold T, the aspect ratio at which a critical rectangle stops being crossed in the scaling limit, has density proportional to η(it)^4 and a Mellin transform equal to the normalised completed L function of the elliptic curve y^2 = x^3 + 1 of conductor 36: the equality in law of T and 1/T is equivalent to the functional equation, and the Riemann hypothesis for the curve says that the characteristic function of log T has only real zeros. The chapter proves that log T is not infinitely divisible and T is not a GGC, and that the Riemann hypothesis for the curve is equivalent to Hankel positivity of zero sums read off the cumulants of log T; a Prony step recovers the low zeros numerically. These are reformulations: no new case of reciprocity and no Riemann hypothesis is proved, and the infinite divisibility and self decomposability of T rest on numerics only.

## From the literature

- Universality and conformal invariance of crossing probabilities (Langlands, Pichet, Pouliot and Saint Aubin 1992; Langlands, Pouliot and Saint Aubin 1994).
- Cardy's formula (Cardy 1992), proved for site percolation on the triangular lattice (Smirnov 2001).
- The derivative of Cardy's formula in the aspect ratio is a multiple of η(ir)^4, linked to a modular parametrisation of the curve y^2 = x^3 + 1 (Kleban and Zagier 2003).
- Eta quotient formulas for annulus crossings (Cardy 2002 and 2006), proved by Sun, Xu and Zhuang (2024); Dubédat (2004) derived a differential equation for the crossing probability from SLE6.
- The law with Mellin transform 2ξ(s) (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); infinitely many zeros on the critical line for L functions of cusp forms (Hafner 1983; Rezvyakova 2010 for Hecke congruence groups).
- Generalized gamma convolutions and their extension (Thorin 1978; Bondesson 1992).

## New results

None of the catalogued results proves a case of the Riemann hypothesis.

- [The Mellin transform of the percolation crossing threshold is the normalised completed L function of y^2 = x^3 + 1](../RESULTS.md#percolation_crossing_threshold_and_the_l_function_of_y2_x3_1) (Theorem 27.5, page 507; proved): The aspect ratio at which a critical percolation rectangle stops being crossed is a random variable whose Mellin transform is, up to a constant, the completed L function of the elliptic curve y^2 = x^3 + 1 of conductor 36, so that its symmetry under inversion is equivalent to the functional equation, its normalising constant gives the central L value in closed form, and the Riemann hypothesis for this curve becomes the statement that the characteristic function of the log threshold has only real zeros, a reformulation that does not prove it. New in this book.
- [Thorin representation of the reciprocal moments of the crossing threshold under the Riemann hypothesis for y^2 = x^3 + 1](../RESULTS.md#thorin_representation_of_reciprocal_crossing_threshold_moments) (Corollary 27.6, page 508; conditional on the Riemann hypothesis for L(E, s), where E is the elliptic curve y^2 = x^3 + 1): Assuming the Riemann hypothesis for the curve y^2 = x^3 + 1, the reciprocal of the moment function of the percolation crossing threshold is the characteristic function of a sum of independent Laplace variables scaled by the reciprocals of the zero ordinates, a symmetric extended generalized gamma convolution with Thorin atoms at the zeros. New in this book.
- [Cardy's annulus crossing probability is not the Laplace transform of a positive measure](../RESULTS.md#annulus_crossing_probability_is_not_a_positive_laplace_transform) (Proposition 27.7, page 510; proved): The probability that an open path crosses an annulus in critical percolation, as a function of the modulus, is not the Laplace transform of a positive measure, so the signed spectral measure in its eta quotient expansion cannot be made positive. New in this book.
- [The log crossing threshold is not infinitely divisible](../RESULTS.md#log_crossing_threshold_is_not_infinitely_divisible) (Theorem 27.8, page 510; proved): The logarithm of the percolation crossing threshold is not infinitely divisible, and so not an extended generalized gamma convolution, because its characteristic function has real zeros, at the ordinates of the zeros of the L function of y^2 = x^3 + 1 that lie on the critical line, and the characteristic function of an infinitely divisible law never vanishes. New in this book.
- [The percolation crossing threshold is not a generalized gamma convolution](../RESULTS.md#crossing_threshold_is_not_a_generalized_gamma_convolution) (Theorem 27.10, page 511; proved): The percolation crossing threshold is not a generalized gamma convolution, since the continuation of its Laplace transform has a real zero between two consecutive poles whose residues have the same sign, and so its density, a multiple of the fourth power of the Dedekind eta function on the imaginary axis, is not hyperbolically completely monotone. New in this book.
- [Numerical self decomposability of the percolation crossing threshold](../RESULTS.md#crossing_threshold_self_decomposable_numerically) (Proposition 27.11, page 512; open): A numerical Laplace inversion indicates, without proof, that the percolation crossing threshold is infinitely divisible and self decomposable, with a positive decreasing Lévy function, although it is provably not a generalized gamma convolution; a proof is posed as an open computational problem. New in this book.
- [Even cumulants of the log crossing threshold are zero sums of the L function of y^2 = x^3 + 1](../RESULTS.md#cumulants_of_log_crossing_threshold_are_zero_sums) (Proposition 27.12, page 513; proved): Unconditionally, the odd cumulants of the logarithm of the percolation crossing threshold vanish and its even cumulants are explicit multiples of the sums of inverse even powers of the zeros of the L function of y^2 = x^3 + 1, so that moments of a crossing probability determine the low lying zeros, and under the Riemann hypothesis these sums are positive and their normalised roots increase to the first zero. New in this book.
- [A criterion for the Riemann hypothesis of y^2 = x^3 + 1 in percolation cumulants](../RESULTS.md#percolation_cumulant_criterion_for_grh_of_y2_x3_1) (Theorem 27.13, page 516; proved): Proves an equivalence and not the hypothesis, namely that the Riemann hypothesis for the L function of y^2 = x^3 + 1 holds if and only if the reciprocal of the moment function of the percolation crossing threshold is the characteristic function of a symmetric extended generalized gamma convolution, if and only if a generating function of the cumulants of the log threshold is a Stieltjes function, if and only if every Hankel matrix of the zero sums computed from those cumulants is positive semidefinite. New in this book.
