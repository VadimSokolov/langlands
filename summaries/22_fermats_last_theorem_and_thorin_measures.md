# Chapter 22. Fermat's Last Theorem and Thorin Measures

Is there a Thorin measure form of Fermat's last theorem? The chapter reads the two kinds of one forms in the proof, weight one modular forms (the Langlands Tunnell theorem that starts Wiles's argument) and weight two forms as holomorphic differentials on X_0(N) (the genus zero of X_0(2) that ends it), through Frobenius label laws, Pólya kernels, generalized gamma convolutions, Thorin measures and hitting times. Weight one newforms are those with finitely supported label laws. The answer has three parts: no Thorin statement is equivalent to Fermat's last theorem in a useful sense; given the modularity of semistable elliptic curves (Wiles; Taylor and Wiles), Ribet's level lowering theorem and Mazur's torsion theorem, the last step is the emptiness of the level two Hecke Thorin measure (one supersingular curve in characteristic 2, class number one of the Hurwitz quaternions); and the proof for prime exponents at least 5 is restated as the incompatibility of that empty measure with a positive Eisenstein Thorin shape of the Frey curve, the shape of a Gamma law and a local density in Koblitz's conjecture. No step of the proof is reproved, and no new case of modularity is proved.

## From the literature

- Modularity of semistable elliptic curves (Wiles 1995; Taylor and Wiles 1995), extended by Breuil, Conrad, Diamond and Taylor (2001); the account of Darmon, Diamond and Taylor (1995).
- The Frey curve and the reduction to modularity (Hellegouarch 1975; Frey 1986; Serre 1987), Ribet's level lowering (Ribet 1990) and level raising (Ribet 1984).
- Mazur's torsion theorem (Mazur 1977), giving irreducibility for Frey curves (Serre 1987).
- The Langlands Tunnell theorem (Langlands 1980; Tunnell 1981); weight one forms and Artin representations (Deligne and Serre 1974; Khare and Wintenberger 2009; Kisin 2009).
- Wilton's congruence for tau modulo 23 (Wilton 1930), explained by Serre (1968).
- Converse theorems (Weil 1967; Conrey and Farmer 1995); Sato Tate (Barnet Lamb, Geraghty, Harris and Taylor 2011).
- Koblitz's conjecture and its local densities (Koblitz 1988; Zywina 2011), with vanishing criteria of Serre (1972) and Katz (1981).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Gamma law of the Eisenstein discrepancy of an elliptic curve](../RESULTS.md#gamma_law_of_the_eisenstein_discrepancy) (Proposition 22.11, page 409; proved): For an elliptic curve and a prime p, the rescaled logarithmic weight that a zeta distributed random integer puts on the primes at which p does not divide the number of points of the curve converges to a Gamma law whose shape, the classical local density at p in Koblitz's conjecture (Koblitz; Zywina), is also the rate at which the prime factors of the integer hit those primes; the reading of this density as the shape of a Gamma law and as a hitting rate is the book's. New in this book.
- [Fermat's last theorem restated as two Thorin statements](../RESULTS.md#fermat_last_theorem_restated_as_two_thorin_statements) (Theorem 22.13, page 412; proved): Given the modularity of semistable elliptic curves (Wiles; Taylor and Wiles), Ribet's level lowering theorem and Mazur's torsion theorem, the known proof of Fermat's last theorem for prime exponents at least five is restated as the incompatibility of a positive Eisenstein Thorin shape of the Frey curve with the empty Hecke Thorin measure at level two; no step of the proof is reproved, and its content stays in those theorems. New in this book.
- [Hasse Weil bound from hitting times on supersingular isogeny graphs](../RESULTS.md#hasse_weil_bound_from_hitting_times) (Open Problem 22.1, page 415; open): Find a probabilistic proof, for instance through hitting times or a heat kernel on the supersingular isogeny graph, of the bound of Eichler and Shimura with Weil on the Hecke eigenvalues of weight two newforms of prime level. New in this book.
- [Taylor Wiles patching as a Gamma process](../RESULTS.md#taylor_wiles_patching_as_a_gamma_process) (Open Problem 22.2, page 415; open): Ask whether the auxiliary primes of the Taylor Wiles patching method can be taken as the atoms, in a fixed Chebotarev class, of the Gamma process of the prime factors of a zeta distributed random integer, with the patched objects as limits in law. New in this book.
- [A probabilistic Mazur theorem for Eisenstein shapes of curves with rational 2 torsion](../RESULTS.md#probabilistic_mazur_theorem_for_eisenstein_shapes) (Open Problem 22.3, page 415; open): Ask for a proof through the Gamma law of the Eisenstein discrepancy and isogeny walks, rather than through Mazur's torsion theorem, that an elliptic curve over the rationals with all its 2 torsion rational has positive Eisenstein shape at every prime at least five. New in this book.
