# Chapter 14. The Thorin Framework for Automorphic L Functions

From *On Langlands Conjectures: Probabilistic Methods* by Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 14. Can the generalized Riemann hypothesis (GRH) for an automorphic L function be restated as positivity of a probability law? For every cuspidal automorphic representation of GL(n) over the rationals the chapter builds a nonnegative self dual completion and reads its zeros in the variable s = z^2, where their squared ordinates become Thorin atoms. Extending the first author's 2018 equivalence for Laguerre Pólya functions, it proves that GRH is equivalent to each of six statements: the reciprocal of the completion is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution (GGC); a Lévy function of the zeros is completely monotone; a logarithmic derivative is a Stieltjes function, or satisfies a Herglotz inequality; the power sums of the atoms, computable from central Taylor coefficients, form a Stieltjes moment sequence; and, for a nonnegative Pólya kernel, so do the rescaled even cumulants of the Pólya variable. It also reads the central order of vanishing as a degenerate Thorin atom, relaxes GRH to parabolic conditions along the de Bruijn heat flow, tests the criteria on eleven L functions, and gives a regularised version of order two for Selberg zeta functions, where it means no exceptional eigenvalue. These equivalences are reformulations and prove no case of GRH.

## From the literature

Generalized gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992) and the reciprocal Xi function (Polson 2017); the Thorin equivalence for Laguerre Pólya functions (Polson 2018); Bernstein's theorem (Bernstein 1929); Stieltjes functions and their Nevanlinna Pick characterisation (Schilling, Song and Vondraček 2012); the Stieltjes moment problem (Stieltjes 1894; Akhiezer 1965) and Hankel criteria for real zeros (Grommer 1914); standard L functions of GL(n) (Godement and Jacquet 1972; Jacquet and Shalika 1976); de Bruijn's heat flow theorem (de Bruijn 1950), Newman's conjecture (Rodgers and Tao 2020; Dobner 2021) and zero dynamics under the heat flow (Csordas, Smith and Varga 1994; Polymath 2019); the rank part of Birch and Swinnerton Dyer in analytic rank at most one (Gross and Zagier 1986; Kolyvagin 1988); the bound 7/64 toward Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture (Kim and Sarnak 2003).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Self dual completion of a cuspidal automorphic L function](../RESULTS.md#self_dual_completion_automorphic_l_function) (Lemma 14.6, page 215; proved): Builds from the L function of any cuspidal automorphic representation of GL(n) over the rationals an even entire completion, real and nonnegative on the real axis and positive at the centre, with all zeros in the open critical strip and a genus zero product in the squared variable. New in this book.
- [Thorin equivalence for even entire functions with zeros in a strip](../RESULTS.md#thorin_equivalence_even_entire_functions) (Theorem 14.7, page 216; proved): For an even entire function of order less than two, real on the real axis, equal to one at the origin and with zeros in a vertical strip, all zeros lie on the imaginary axis exactly when any one of several positivity properties holds (a generalized gamma convolution, a completely monotone Lévy function, a Stieltjes or Herglotz property of the logarithmic derivative, a Stieltjes moment sequence of power sums), extending an equivalence of the first author's 2018 paper for Laguerre Pólya functions. New in this book.
- [Thorin criteria for the generalized Riemann hypothesis of automorphic L functions](../RESULTS.md#thorin_criteria_generalized_riemann_hypothesis_automorphic) (Theorem 14.8, page 219; proved): The generalized Riemann hypothesis for the L function of a cuspidal automorphic representation of GL(n) over the rationals, and the Riemann hypothesis for zeta, are each equivalent to several positivity statements, among them that the reciprocal of the self dual completion, read in the squared variable, is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin measure sits at the squared ordinates of the zeros, and a Hankel criterion computable from central derivatives; the theorem is an equivalence that extends the first author's 2018 paper, and it proves no case of either hypothesis. New in this book.
- [Pólya kernel of a cuspidal automorphic L function](../RESULTS.md#polya_kernel_automorphic_l_function) (Lemma 14.12, page 221; proved): The L function of every cuspidal automorphic representation of GL(n) over the rationals has a real even kernel with doubly exponential decay whose two sided Laplace transform is its normalised self dual completion, although the kernel need not be positive. New in this book.
- [Cumulant criterion for the generalized Riemann hypothesis via the Pólya variable](../RESULTS.md#cumulant_criterion_generalized_riemann_hypothesis) (Proposition 14.13, page 222; proved): When the Pólya kernel of an automorphic L function is nonnegative, the even cumulants of the associated symmetric random variable are, up to sign and scale, the power sums of the Thorin atoms, so the generalized Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to a Stieltjes moment property of the rescaled cumulants. New in this book.
- [Central order of vanishing as a degenerate Thorin atom at the origin](../RESULTS.md#central_order_of_vanishing_as_degenerate_thorin_atom) (Proposition 14.14, page 222; conditional on the generalized Riemann hypothesis for L(s, pi), for the Laplace transform representation only (the slope limit is unconditional)): The order of vanishing of a self dual automorphic L function at the centre, such as the analytic rank of an elliptic curve, appears as a Thorin atom of mass half the order escaping to the origin, unconditionally as a log log slope and, under the generalized Riemann hypothesis, in a Laplace transform representation. New in this book.
- [Gaussian tilting of the Pólya kernel and the Thorin property along the heat flow](../RESULTS.md#gaussian_tilting_polya_kernel_thorin_property) (Theorem 14.17, page 225; proved): Tilting the Pólya kernel of an automorphic L function by a Gaussian factor narrows the strip containing the zeros and makes them all real once the tilt is large enough (de Bruijn's theorem, the first part), and the book adds that, when the kernel is nonnegative, the reciprocal of the tilted transform in the squared variable is then the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution, unconditionally for every tilt of at least one eighth. New in this book.
- [Regularised Thorin equivalence for even entire functions of order two](../RESULTS.md#regularised_thorin_equivalence_order_two) (Proposition 14.20, page 229; proved): For even entire functions of order at most two whose zeros have summable inverse fourth powers, such as the completed Selberg zeta function of a compact hyperbolic surface, all Thorin atoms are positive exactly when the reciprocal is the Laplace transform of a centred sum of exponential variables, which for the surface means no exceptional Laplace eigenvalue (for arithmetic surfaces Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture, which this equivalence does not prove). New in this book.
