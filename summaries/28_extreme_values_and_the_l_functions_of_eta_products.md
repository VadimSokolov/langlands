# Chapter 28. Extreme Values and the L Functions of Eta Products

On the imaginary axis the product in the Dedekind eta function is the distribution function of a maximum of independent exponential clocks. A Mellin transform argument then gives the extreme value representation, new in this book: the L function of Ramanujan's Delta function, and of every eta product ∏ η(mτ)^(r_m) with nonnegative integer r_m and Σ m r_m = 24 (among them the thirty multiplicative eta products), is entire and equals at every complex point the expectation of a regularised incomplete gamma function at such a maximum. For real positive s it is the probability that an independent gamma variable beats the maximum, so these L values lie strictly between 0 and 1 and increase to 1, and the central L value of the elliptic curve 11a, an Artin L value, Catalan's constant and π/4 become probabilities. Modularity becomes self similarity of the law, the Pólya kernel of Delta a Bayesian posterior, and Jacobi's triple product a link to the Kolmogorov distribution; a Monte Carlo estimator follows. The Riemann hypothesis for Delta is restated only as a reformulation, and the representation does not locate zeros.

## From the literature

- Gumbel limits for maxima (Fisher and Tippett 1928, Gnedenko 1943); Rényi's representation (Rényi 1953).
- Entire continuation of L(s, Delta) and its incomplete gamma expansion (Hecke 1936); the weight one eta product of level 23 as an Artin L function (Hecke 1937).
- Multiplicative eta products (Dummit, Kisilevsky and McKay 1985) and multiplicative eta quotients (Martin 1996).
- The newform of 11a (Eichler 1954, Shimura 1971), the Birch and Swinnerton Dyer formula (Birch and Swinnerton Dyer 1965), and rank zero from analytic rank zero (Kolyvagin 1988, Gross and Zagier 1986).
- Riemann's xi as a moment of a Brownian excursion functional (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); zeros off the line without an Euler product (Davenport and Heilbronn 1936); real zero criteria (Pólya 1926, de Bruijn 1950, Newman 1976).
- Kolmogorov's law (Kolmogorov 1933); diffusion passage times as sums of exponentials (Kent 1980).

## New results

The Notes claim the representation and its consequences as the authors' own.

- [Product formula of the Dedekind eta function as the distribution function of a maximum of exponential clocks](../RESULTS.md#eta_product_as_distribution_function_of_exponential_maximum) (Proposition 28.1, page 519; proved): The infinite product in the Dedekind eta function, read on the imaginary axis, is the distribution function of the maximum of independent exponential clocks with rates proportional to the positive integers, with explicit tails and a density given by the weight two Eisenstein series. New in this book.
- [Extreme value representation of the L function of Ramanujan's Delta](../RESULTS.md#l_function_of_ramanujan_delta_as_extreme_value_expectation) (Theorem 28.2, page 521; proved): At every complex point the L function of Ramanujan's Delta function equals the expectation of a regularised incomplete gamma function evaluated at a maximum of independent exponential clocks, and at positive real points it is the probability that an independent gamma variable beats that maximum. New in this book.
- [L values of Ramanujan's Delta at positive real points lie strictly between 0 and 1](../RESULTS.md#real_l_values_of_ramanujan_delta_lie_in_unit_interval) (Corollary 28.3, page 522; proved): Every value of the L function of Ramanujan's Delta function at a positive real point lies strictly between zero and one, being the probability of an event that has positive probability together with its complement. New in this book.
- [Strict monotonicity of the L function of Ramanujan's Delta on the positive real axis](../RESULTS.md#l_function_of_ramanujan_delta_increases_to_one) (Corollary 28.4, page 522; proved): On the positive real axis the L function of Ramanujan's Delta function increases strictly and tends to one, proved by a coupling of gamma variables. New in this book.
- [Modularity of Ramanujan's Delta as self similarity of an extreme value law](../RESULTS.md#modularity_of_delta_as_self_similarity_of_extreme_value_law) (Proposition 28.5, page 523; proved): The modular transformation of Ramanujan's Delta function becomes an exact self similarity of the law of the maximum of exponential clocks behind it, which exchanges the two tails of that law. New in this book.
- [Pólya kernel of Ramanujan's Delta as a Bayesian posterior](../RESULTS.md#polya_kernel_of_delta_as_posterior_law) (Proposition 28.6, page 524; proved): The Pólya kernel of Ramanujan's Delta function is the law of the logarithm of a gamma variable of shape six and rate two pi, conditioned to exceed the maximum of exponential clocks behind Delta, so its moment generating function is a ratio of completed L values. New in this book.
- [Extreme value representation of L functions of eta products](../RESULTS.md#extreme_value_representation_of_eta_product_l_functions) (Theorem 28.7, page 525; proved): For every eta product, that is every product of the functions eta(m tau) raised to nonnegative integer powers r_m with the sum of m r_m equal to 24 (the thirty multiplicative eta products among them), the L function is entire and equals at every complex point the expectation of a regularised incomplete gamma function evaluated at an explicit maximum of exponential clocks, so its values at positive real points are probabilities strictly between zero and one that increase strictly to one. New in this book.
- [Squared Kolmogorov variable as a maximum of exponentials and an identity in law with the eta extreme value](../RESULTS.md#kolmogorov_distribution_as_maximum_of_exponentials) (Proposition 28.9, page 529; proved): The square of the largest absolute value of a Brownian bridge, whose law is Kolmogorov's, is in law a maximum of independent exponential variables, and Jacobi's triple product becomes an identity in law linking it to the extreme value law behind the Dedekind eta function. New in this book.
- [Brownian or Bessel functional with the extreme value law behind the Dedekind eta function](../RESULTS.md#brownian_functional_with_eta_extreme_value_law) (Open Problem 28.1, page 529; open): Asks for a natural Brownian or Bessel functional whose law is the extreme value law behind the Dedekind eta function or Ramanujan's Delta function, or a proof that no first passage or last exit time of a one dimensional diffusion has it. New in this book.
