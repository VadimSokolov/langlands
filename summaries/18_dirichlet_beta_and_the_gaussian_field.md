# Chapter 18. Dirichlet Beta and the Gaussian Field

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *On Langlands Conjectures: Probabilistic Methods* by Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 18. Source: https://github.com/VadimSokolov/langlands.

The chapter works out the Thorin framework of the Riemann xi function for the Dirichlet beta function, the L function of the character modulo 4 of the Gaussian field Q(i). Its Pólya kernel is the eta cube theta series, positive by Jacobi's identity, but its Lévy measure is signed, so Thorin positivity is arithmetic rather than termwise. The chapter recalls the closed form of the Thorin density, the generalized gamma convolution (GGC) property for basepoints at least one and the equivalence of the generalized Riemann hypothesis (GRH) for beta with Thorin positivity; shows by Kronecker's theorem that bounds using only the sizes of prime atoms are sharp; and writes the Thorin density of beta as that of the Dedekind zeta function of Q(i) minus that of zeta. Its own results, some unconditional only below about the height to which GRH for beta is verified, are positivity on the real axis, localisation of negative Thorin density near zeros off the line, a comparison lemma with sharp constant 1 + √2, the minimum of the logarithmic derivative on the real axis (an analogue of Lagarias's theorem), and the Thorin measure as the argument. None proves a Riemann hypothesis.

## From the literature

- Jacobi's identity for the eta cube and Euler's pentagonal identity; the theta series proof of the functional equation (Iwaniec and Kowalski 2004).
- The Riemann hypothesis as positivity of the logarithmic derivative of xi right of the critical line (Hinkkanen 1997; Lagarias 1999), and its minimum on vertical lines at the real axis (Lagarias 1999; Garunkštis 2000, 2002; Broughan 2009).
- The grouping of zeros off the line (Levinson and Montgomery 1974).
- Verifications of GRH for small conductors (Platt 2016) and of the Riemann hypothesis (Platt and Trudgian 2021).
- The Pólya variable of xi is not infinitely divisible (Roynette and Yor 2005).
- From the authors' papers: the argument form for xi (Polson 2017); the sine squared lemma and a complex character modulo 5 where the minimum property fails (Polson 2018).

## New results

Polson (2018) first stated for real primitive characters the closed form of the Thorin density, the GGC property above basepoint one and the equivalence of GRH with the GGC property.

- [Lévy form of the completed Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_levy_form) (Theorem 18.4, page 304; proved): To the right of the critical strip the logarithm of the completed Dirichlet beta function has a Lévy Khintchine form whose Lévy measure is signed, a positive archimedean density plus prime atoms carrying the signs of the character modulo 4. New in this book.
- [Signed sine squared lemma for Thorin densities](../RESULTS.md#signed_sine_squared_lemma) (Lemma 18.6, page 304; proved): The sine squared lemma of Polson (2018), with a first step of Roynette and Yor, which turns the Lévy measure of a function into the Thorin density of its shifted reciprocal, extends linearly to signed Lévy measures once the positivity conclusion is dropped. New in this book.
- [Thorin density of the reciprocal completed Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_thorin_density) (Theorem 18.7, page 305; proved): To the right of the critical strip the shifted reciprocal of the completed Dirichlet beta function has an explicit Thorin representation whose density is the real part of the logarithmic derivative on a vertical line divided by an explicit factor. First stated in Polson (2018, version 9 of 2026), Riemann, Thorin, van Dantzig pairs, Wald couples and Hadamard factorisation, arXiv:1804.10043.
- [GGC property of the shifted reciprocal Dirichlet beta function for basepoints at least one](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_ggc_basepoints_at_least_one) (Theorem 18.8, page 306; proved): Unconditionally, for every basepoint at least one, the shifted reciprocal of the completed Dirichlet beta function is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution with drift zero and a positive Thorin density; for basepoints above one this is a case of a theorem of Polson (2018). New in this book.
- [Termwise Thorin positivity threshold for the Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_termwise_thorin_threshold) (Proposition 18.9, page 307; proved): Bounding the prime atoms of the Dirichlet beta function by their absolute values proves positivity of its Thorin density at every height only for basepoints above an explicit threshold close to 2.28. New in this book.
- [GRH for the Dirichlet beta function as Thorin positivity](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_grh_as_thorin_positivity) (Theorem 18.10, page 308; proved): The generalized Riemann hypothesis for the Dirichlet beta function is equivalent to the generalized gamma convolution property of its shifted reciprocals at every basepoint right of the critical line, and to positivity of the real part of its logarithmic derivative there (the criterion of Hinkkanen and Lagarias), a reformulation and not a proof. First stated in Polson (2018, version 9 of 2026), Riemann, Thorin, van Dantzig pairs, Wald couples and Hadamard factorisation, arXiv:1804.10043.
- [Thorin domination in the Gaussian field](../RESULTS.md#gaussian_field_thorin_domination) (Theorem 18.13, page 310; proved): Through the factorisation of the Dedekind zeta function of the Gaussian field, the Thorin density of the Dirichlet beta function is that of the Gaussian field minus that of the Riemann zeta function, and the generalized Riemann hypothesis for beta is equivalent to the first dominating the second on every line right of the critical line. New in this book.
- [Kronecker sharpness of termwise Thorin bounds for the Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_kronecker_sharpness) (Proposition 18.14, page 310; proved): By Kronecker's theorem, bounds on the Thorin density of the Dirichlet beta function that use only the sizes of the prime atoms are sharp, so they prove positivity on a line only above a height that grows exponentially as the basepoint approaches one. New in this book.
- [Unconditional Thorin positivity of the Dirichlet beta function near the real axis](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_thorin_positivity_near_real_axis) (Theorem 18.15, page 311; proved): On every line right of the critical line the Thorin density of the Dirichlet beta function is positive near the origin, unconditionally, because the logarithm of the completed function is the cumulant generating function of a symmetric law with a positive density. New in this book.
- [Localisation of negative Thorin density for the Dirichlet beta function below the verified height](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_negative_thorin_windows) (Corollary 18.17, page 312; proved): Read as a statement on Thorin densities, the grouping of zeros of Levinson and Montgomery shows that the Thorin density of the Dirichlet beta function is positive on every line right of the critical line up to half a unit below the height to which its generalized Riemann hypothesis has been verified, and that a failure of the hypothesis could make it negative only in windows of half width less than one half around ordinates of zeros off the line. New in this book.
- [Comparison lemma for pairs of zeros with sharp constant one plus root two](../RESULTS.md#zero_pair_comparison_lemma_sharp_constant) (Lemma 18.18, page 312; proved): An elementary lemma with a sharp constant on how the contribution of a symmetric pair of zeros to the real part of a logarithmic derivative, divided by the distance to the critical line, varies with that distance. New in this book.
- [Comparison of the logarithmic derivative across vertical lines](../RESULTS.md#log_derivative_comparison_across_vertical_lines) (Theorem 18.19, page 312; proved): At heights not too close to a zero off the critical line, and so unconditionally at heights up to 1.21 below the height to which the generalized Riemann hypothesis for beta has been verified, the real part of the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function divided by the distance to the critical line does not increase as the line moves right, and the same holds for the Riemann xi function and for real primitive characters. New in this book.
- [Explicit lower bound for the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_explicit_log_derivative_lower_bound) (Corollary 18.20, page 313; proved): At heights not too close to a zero off the critical line, the real part of the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function on lines right of the critical line has an explicit lower bound that grows like a multiple of the logarithm of the height. New in this book.
- [Minimum on the real axis of the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_minimum_on_real_axis) (Theorem 18.21, page 313; proved): On each line right of the critical line with real part at most 3.15, the real part of the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function is smallest on the real axis, unconditionally at heights up to 1.21 below the height to which its generalized Riemann hypothesis has been verified, and that hypothesis is equivalent to this minimum property at every height on the lines up to real part one. New in this book.
- [Thorin measure of the Dirichlet beta function as its argument](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_thorin_measure_is_argument) (Theorem 18.22, page 314; proved): Extending a theorem of Polson (2017) for the Riemann xi function, the cumulative Thorin measure of the shifted completed Dirichlet beta function on a zero free vertical line equals its argument divided by pi, which gives a Riemann von Mangoldt type asymptotic and, under the generalized Riemann hypothesis, the zero counting function in the limit at the critical line. New in this book.
- [GRH for the Dirichlet beta function as a monotone argument](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_grh_as_monotone_argument) (Corollary 18.23, page 315; proved): The generalized Riemann hypothesis for the Dirichlet beta function is equivalent to the argument of its completed function being nondecreasing along every zero free vertical line right of the critical line (for the Riemann xi function, part of a theorem of Polson 2017), a reformulation and not a proof. New in this book.
- [Eta cube kernel of the Dirichlet beta function is not completely monotone](../RESULTS.md#eta_cube_kernel_not_completely_monotone) (Proposition 18.24, page 316; proved): Neither the one sided Pólya kernel of the Dirichlet beta function, divided by its variable, nor the cube of the eta function on the imaginary axis is completely monotone, so the kernel is not a positive mixture of gamma densities of shape two. New in this book.
- [Thorin domination, localisation and comparison for real primitive characters](../RESULTS.md#real_characters_thorin_domination_and_comparison) (Theorem 18.25, page 317; proved): The Thorin decomposition through the quadratic field, the generalized gamma convolution property for basepoints at least one (above one a theorem of Polson 2018), the domination criterion for the generalized Riemann hypothesis, the localisation of negative density by the grouping of zeros of Levinson and Montgomery, and the comparison across vertical lines extend from the Dirichlet beta function to every real primitive character, as does positivity near the real axis when the theta kernel of the character is positive. New in this book.
- [Coupling of the Thorin variables of the Gaussian field below basepoint one](../RESULTS.md#gaussian_field_thorin_coupling_below_one) (Open Problem 18.1, page 318; open): Asks for a construction from the Gaussian primes of the splitting of the Thorin variable of the Gaussian field into Riemann zeta and Dirichlet beta parts that could survive to the left of basepoint one, using the phases of the prime atoms. New in this book.
- [Lower bound for the decrease of the argument in a negative window](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_negative_window_argument_bound) (Open Problem 18.2, page 318; open): Asks for a lower bound on how much a hypothetical pair of zeros off the critical line makes the argument of the completed Dirichlet beta function decrease, strong enough to be contradicted by a mean value estimate. New in this book.
- [Minimum on the real axis on all lines to the right](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_beta_minimum_on_all_lines) (Open Problem 18.3, page 318; open): Asks to extend the minimum on the real axis of the logarithmic derivative of the completed Dirichlet beta function to every line right of the critical line below the verified height, and to every height on the lines with real part at least one. New in this book.
- [Positivity on the reciprocal side from eta products](../RESULTS.md#eta_reciprocal_side_positivity) (Open Problem 18.4, page 318; open): Asks whether the complete monotonicity of the reciprocals of the eta cube and eta products passes, through incomplete Mellin transforms, to a positive representation on the reciprocal side of the completed Dirichlet beta function or of the L function of the character modulo 12. New in this book.
