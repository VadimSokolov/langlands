# Chapter 20. Reciprocity through the Swap

From *On Langlands Conjectures: Probabilistic Methods* by Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 20. Source: https://github.com/VadimSokolov/langlands.

How much of Langlands reciprocity, the equality of Galois and automorphic L functions, can be stated as probability about Frobenius data? Quadratic reciprocity, Gauss's sign and the Schaar Landsberg reciprocity for Gauss sums are rederived from the characteristic function of the normal law. On a Frobenius probability space (Dirichlet's measure), reciprocity is almost sure equality, Chebotarev and Rankin Selberg become orthonormality, and reciprocity in law gives uniqueness and irreducibility. Labelling the prime factors of a zeta distributed random integer by equidistributed Frobenius data gives a Gamma process limit: Frobenius functionals converge to generalized gamma convolutions (GGC), reciprocity laws become vanishing Thorin measures, a zero two law holds (conjectured in the mixed case), and the drift of the split primes toward their Gamma limit is Ihara's Euler Kronecker constant over the degree, up to finitely many Euler factors and a prime power term. The Thorin framework (Pólya kernels, explicit formula, van Dantzig pairs, Wald couples) gives a kernel symmetry form of Hecke's converse theorem, a Gamma shape test of irreducibility, a complete monotonicity criterion for Artin's conjecture with GRH from a Galois heat trace, rigidity at a single prime and a Thorin converse theorem at level one. None of this proves a new case of reciprocity, Artin's conjecture or GRH: the criteria restate them or give sufficient conditions.

## From the literature

- Reciprocity for quadratic Gauss sums (Schaar 1850, Landsberg 1893), Gauss's sign (Gauss 1811), quadratic reciprocity from the theta transformation (Dym and McKean 1972), generalized Gauss sums (Berndt, Evans and Williams 1998).
- The Chebotarev density theorem (Chebotarev 1926) via Brauer induction (Brauer 1947); the Rankin Selberg pole, strong multiplicity one (Jacquet and Shalika 1981; Shahidi 1981; Jacquet, Piatetski Shapiro and Shalika 1983; Moeglin and Waldspurger 1989).
- Prime factors of random integers (Billingsley 1972; Lloyd 1984), the Gamma process (Kingman 1993), generalized gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992), Euler Kronecker constants (Ihara 2006).
- Converse theorems (Hecke 1936; Weil 1967; Jacquet and Langlands 1970; Cogdell and Piatetski Shapiro 1994); Weil's explicit formula and positivity criterion (Weil 1952, 1972).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Reciprocity in law between Artin and cuspidal automorphic representations](../RESULTS.md#reciprocity_in_law) (Corollary 20.15, page 346; proved): On a probability space of prime powers weighted by Dirichlet's measure, asymptotic agreement in mean square of Frobenius traces with Hecke eigenvalues follows from reciprocity, determines the partner up to isomorphism on either side, forces irreducibility and gives agreement within any tolerance outside a set of primes of density zero, but asserts no existence. New in this book.
- [Congruence conditions see exactly the abelian part of an Artin representation](../RESULTS.md#congruences_see_exactly_the_abelian_part) (Proposition 20.16, page 347; proved): The limiting conditional mean of the trace of an irreducible Artin representation given a congruence condition modulo N on the prime power is nonzero only for one dimensional representations that factor through the field of N th roots of unity, a reformulation of Chebotarev's theorem for the compositum with a cyclotomic field in which the Kronecker Weber theorem becomes measurability with respect to congruences. New in this book.
- [Gamma process limit of the Frobenius labelled factorisation of a random integer](../RESULTS.md#frobenius_labelled_factorisation_gamma_process_limit) (Theorem 20.18, page 350; proved): As the parameter of a zeta distributed random integer decreases to one, the logarithms of its prime power factors, multiplied by the parameter minus one and labelled by any equidistributed sequence of points such as Frobenius classes or Sato Tate angles, converge in law to the jumps of a standard Gamma subordinator carrying independent labels drawn from the equidistribution law, and the labelled logarithm converges in law to a Gamma random measure, so that equidistribution becomes a Gamma process. New in this book.
- [Chebotarev as a Gamma process with Dirichlet class shares and an arithmetic arcsine law](../RESULTS.md#chebotarev_gamma_process_and_arithmetic_arcsine_law) (Corollary 20.20, page 352; proved): The parts of the logarithm of a zeta distributed random integer carried by the primes of each Frobenius class are independent and asymptotically Gamma with shape the Chebotarev density, their shares are asymptotically Dirichlet and independent of the exponential total, and for a quadratic character the share carried by the inert primes converges in law to the arcsine law. New in this book.
- [Frobenius functionals of a zeta random integer converge to generalized gamma convolutions](../RESULTS.md#frobenius_functionals_converge_to_generalized_gamma_convolutions) (Theorem 20.21, page 353; proved): Weighting the rescaled logarithms of the prime power factors of a zeta distributed random integer by a nonnegative continuous function of equidistributed labels, such as Frobenius data, gives in the limit a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin measure is an image of the label law, and, when the set where the weight is positive is a continuity set of that law, the probability that the weighted sum vanishes is the parameter minus one raised to the Thorin mass, to leading logarithmic order. New in this book.
- [Thorin criteria for reciprocity laws as vanishing of Thorin measures](../RESULTS.md#thorin_criteria_for_reciprocity) (Theorem 20.22, page 354; proved): For pairs of Artin representations, abelian characters, quadratic reciprocity and pairs of elliptic curves, the squared discrepancy of Frobenius data gives in the limit a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin measure vanishes exactly when the two kinds of data agree, which restates Kronecker Weber and quadratic reciprocity and computes the shape of their failure, for example a scaled chi squared law when the sign factor is omitted from quadratic reciprocity. New in this book.
- [Zero two law for the discrepancy subordinator of two Artin or two cuspidal representations](../RESULTS.md#zero_two_law_for_frobenius_discrepancy_subordinators) (Theorem 20.23, page 356; proved): When the squared discrepancy between the Frobenius traces of two Artin representations, or between the Hecke eigenvalues of two cuspidal automorphic representations, sets the intensity of a random integer, its rescaled logarithm converges in law to a Gamma law whose integer shape is the squared distance between the two, which is zero or two when both are irreducible Artin representations or both are cuspidal. New in this book.
- [Mixed zero two law conjecture for an Artin and a cuspidal automorphic representation](../RESULTS.md#mixed_zero_two_law_conjecture) (Conjecture 20.24, page 357; open): The book conjectures that for an irreducible Artin representation and a cuspidal automorphic representation of the same dimension the rescaled discrepancy subordinator has as limit either a point mass at zero or a Gamma law of shape two, the former exactly when their local factors agree at almost all primes, which is the orthogonality half of reciprocity and asserts no existence. New in this book.
- [Mixed zero two law for automorphic Artin representations and unconditional moment bounds](../RESULTS.md#mixed_zero_two_law_known_cases_and_moment_bounds) (Proposition 20.25, page 357; proved): The mixed zero two law holds whenever the Artin representation is automorphic, hence in dimension one, for odd two dimensional representations and for two dimensional representations with solvable image, the rescaled discrepancy has bounded first and second moments unconditionally, and a limit concentrated at zero implies reciprocity in law. New in this book.
- [Euler Kronecker drift in the Gamma limit of Chebotarev classes](../RESULTS.md#euler_kronecker_drift_of_chebotarev_gamma_limit) (Theorem 20.26, page 358; proved): The rescaled part of the logarithm of a zeta distributed random integer carried by the primes of a Frobenius class approaches its Gamma limit at a rate linear in the parameter minus one, with an explicit drift built from logarithmic derivatives of Artin L functions at one and a prime power term, and for the primes that split completely the drift is Ihara's Euler Kronecker constant of the field, with the Euler factors at the excluded primes removed, divided by the degree, plus a nonnegative term. New in this book.
- [Hecke's criterion as the symmetry of a Pólya kernel built from Frobenius traces](../RESULTS.md#hecke_criterion_as_polya_kernel_symmetry) (Proposition 20.27, page 361; proved): A restatement of Hecke's theorem in which the functional equation of a Dirichlet series with polynomially bounded coefficients becomes the evenness or oddness of its Pólya kernel, so that at level one the modularity of Galois data is the symmetry of one function on the line built from Frobenius traces. New in this book.
- [Gamma shape of the Rankin Selberg subordinator detects irreducibility of an Artin representation](../RESULTS.md#gamma_shape_detects_irreducibility) (Theorem 20.28, page 363; proved): The rescaled Rankin Selberg subordinator of an Artin representation, built from squared Frobenius traces, converges to a Gamma law whose shape is the sum of the squared multiplicities of its irreducible constituents, so the representation is irreducible exactly when the limit is exponential, the Galois counterpart of cuspidality on the automorphic side. New in this book.
- [Complete monotonicity of the Galois heat trace as a criterion for holomorphy and GRH](../RESULTS.md#galois_heat_trace_complete_monotonicity_criterion) (Theorem 20.30, page 364; proved): For a self dual L function with meromorphic continuation of finite order and a functional equation, a heat trace computed from Frobenius traces, the conductor and the gamma factor equals half the sum over zeros minus half the sum over poles of a Gaussian in the ordinate, and it is completely monotone exactly when the L function is entire with all zeros on the critical line, a restricted form of Weil's positivity criterion that reformulates Artin's conjecture together with GRH and proves neither. New in this book.
- [The Galois heat trace detects a change of Frobenius data at a single prime](../RESULTS.md#galois_heat_trace_sees_every_prime) (Proposition 20.31, page 366; proved): Changing the local factors of an L function that is entire with all zeros on the critical line at a nonempty set of primes thin enough that the sum of log p over the square root of p converges, a single prime included, while keeping the conductor, the gamma factor and Satake parameters of absolute value at most one, destroys the complete monotonicity of the Galois heat trace, unlike any statement about the distribution of Frobenius data. New in this book.
- [Euler half line Thorin criterion for holomorphy, functional equation and GRH](../RESULTS.md#euler_half_line_thorin_criterion) (Theorem 20.32, page 367; proved): If the reciprocal of a self dual completed Euler product, read on the half line beyond one after a square root change of variable, is a constant times the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin measure is a sum of point masses of positive integer weight, then the completed L function is entire, satisfies its functional equation, is bounded in vertical strips and has all zeros on the critical line, and conversely for an entire completion of order one with nonzero central value and all zeros on the critical line. New in this book.
- [Thorin converse theorem at level one](../RESULTS.md#thorin_converse_theorem_level_one) (Corollary 20.33, page 368; proved): A Thorin form of Hecke's converse theorem in which self dual Galois data of conductor one, with the gamma factor of a weight divisible by four and the Ramanujan bound, come from a cusp form for the full modular group satisfying GRH whenever the normalised reciprocal Euler product is a constant times the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin measure is a sum of point masses of positive integer weight, a hypothesis stated on the Euler half line and stronger than reciprocity since it contains GRH. New in this book.
- [Unconditional generalized gamma convolution family from the Rankin Selberg square of an Artin representation](../RESULTS.md#rankin_selberg_square_ggc_family) (Proposition 20.34, page 369; proved): For every Artin representation and every base point beyond one, the normalised reciprocal of the completed Rankin Selberg square is an explicit exponential factor times the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution whose Thorin density is the sine squared transport of a measure built from squared Frobenius traces plus a gamma factor term, so positivity holds unconditionally on the Euler half line. New in this book.
- [Thorin converse theorem for twists that are not self dual](../RESULTS.md#thorin_converse_theorem_for_twists_problem) (Open Problem 20.1, page 371; open): The book asks for a version of its Euler half line Thorin criterion for twists that are not self dual, strong enough to combine with the converse theorem of Cogdell and Piatetski Shapiro. New in this book.
- [Descending the base point of transported Thorin densities of the Rankin Selberg square](../RESULTS.md#descending_the_base_point_problem) (Open Problem 20.2, page 371; open): The book asks whether the transported Thorin densities of the Rankin Selberg square of an automorphic Artin representation, with the drift split off, stay nonnegative for base points between the centre and the edge of the critical strip, and which features of Frobenius data control their sign. New in this book.
- [Signed Thorin measures separating Artin's conjecture from GRH](../RESULTS.md#signed_thorin_measures_artin_conjecture_problem) (Open Problem 20.3, page 371; open): The book asks for a property of Frobenius data on the Euler half line that forces the integer weights of the signed Thorin measure of an Artin L function to be positive, which is Artin's conjecture, without forcing its atoms to be real, which is GRH. New in this book.
- [A Fejér summation for primes making the Galois heat trace positive](../RESULTS.md#fejer_summation_for_primes_problem) (Open Problem 20.4, page 372; open): The book asks for positive Fejér type weights on prime powers under which positivity of the weighted Galois heat trace follows from Frobenius data alone, as Fejér positivity does for the heat trace of the circle, which through the complete monotonicity criterion would give holomorphy and GRH. New in this book.
- [Reciprocity in law versus reciprocity](../RESULTS.md#reciprocity_in_law_versus_reciprocity_problem) (Open Problem 20.5, page 372; open): The book asks to show that an irreducible Artin representation reciprocal in law with a cuspidal automorphic representation agrees with it at all but finitely many primes, a strong multiplicity one statement across the Galois and automorphic sides. New in this book.
- [The mixed zero two law under meromorphy and for an even icosahedral representation](../RESULTS.md#mixed_zero_two_law_problem) (Open Problem 20.6, page 372; open): The book asks to derive the dichotomy of its mixed zero two law from meromorphy of the mixed Rankin Selberg L function near the edge of the critical strip, to reduce the rest of the conjecture to reciprocity in law, and to prove the conjecture for one even icosahedral Artin representation. New in this book.
- [Joint marked Gamma processes of an Artin and a cuspidal representation](../RESULTS.md#joint_marked_processes_problem) (Open Problem 20.7, page 372; open): The book asks whether the joint limit of the Frobenius labelled Gamma processes of an Artin representation and a cuspidal automorphic representation exists, and whether its label law either sits on the set where the two traces agree or gives their squared discrepancy a Thorin mass bounded below in terms of the dimension. New in this book.
- [Signs of the Euler Kronecker drifts of Chebotarev classes](../RESULTS.md#signs_of_euler_kronecker_drifts_problem) (Open Problem 20.8, page 372; open): The book asks to characterise the Galois number fields whose split primes approach their Gamma limit from above, which requires a negative Euler Kronecker constant, and to relate the signs of the drifts of the Frobenius classes to Chebotarev biases and races. New in this book.
