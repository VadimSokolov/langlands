# Chapter 24. Random Integers and the Poisson Dirichlet Law

How do the prime factors of a random integer, coloured by their Frobenius classes, approach Chebotarev's law? The chapter relates random integers to the Poisson Dirichlet law and Ferguson's Dirichlet process through the zeta distributed random integer and the Gamma process limit of its labelled factorisation. It shows that the normalised labelled factorisation converges to the Dirichlet process with Haar base measure, so that Frobenius functionals converge to an exponential variable times a Dirichlet mean (the Markov Krein identity); that integers weighted by a power of zeta, or by an Euler product with nonnegative logarithmic coefficients, give the whole Ewens family, with Ewens parameter the exponent of the singularity at one; that a size biased prime factor of a uniform integer below x lies in a class with probability the Chebotarev density plus an explicit term of order 1/log x, while the share of distinct prime factors converges only at rate 1/log log x; and that empty classes bound from below the Kolmogorov distance of class shares to their Beta limits. Mertens constants per class are expressed through L(1, χ) and its logarithmic derivative.

## From the literature

The Poisson Dirichlet law (Kingman 1975); Billingsley's theorem (Billingsley 1972; Knuth and Trabb Pardo 1976), its proof by size biased sampling (Donnelly and Grimmett 1993); Dickman's function (Dickman 1930); the Erdős Kac theorem (Erdős and Kac 1940) and the Sathe Selberg formula (Sathe 1953; Selberg 1954); the zeta random integer (Lloyd 1984); the Dirichlet process (Ferguson 1973) and the identity for Dirichlet means (Cifarelli and Regazzini 1990); the Frobenius class of the largest prime factor (Dawsey 2017); Mertens constants per Chebotarev class (Bardestani and Freiberg 2013); frobenian sets (Serre 1976); Poisson Dirichlet limits for weighted integers (Elboim and Gorodetsky 2024) and arithmetic semigroups (Arratia, Kochman and Miller 2014) and the unlabelled limits for the zeta power random integer (Sun 2026); generalized gamma convolutions (Thorin 1977; Bondesson 1992, 2015).

## New results

New are the labelled limits, the size biased expansion, the independence of class and share, and the empty class atom with its Kolmogorov bound.

- [Chebotarev as a Dirichlet process for the labelled factorisation of a random integer](../RESULTS.md#chebotarev_as_a_dirichlet_process) (Theorem 24.3, page 447; proved): As the parameter of a zeta distributed random integer decreases to one, its prime factorisation labelled by Frobenius classes, normalised, converges to Ferguson's Dirichlet process with Haar measure as base measure, and every Frobenius functional converges to an exponential variable times a Dirichlet mean. New in this book.
- [Labelled Ewens family from the random integers weighted by the coefficients of powers of zeta](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_process_limit_of_the_zeta_power_random_integer) (Theorem 24.4, page 450; proved): For random integers weighted by the Dirichlet coefficients of a power of the zeta function, the labelled prime factorisation converges to a Gamma random measure and its normalisation to a Dirichlet process, so powers of zeta realise the whole Ewens family with Frobenius labels; the unlabelled limits for this random integer are due to W. Sun (2026). New in this book.
- [Expansion in 1/log x of the Frobenius class of a size biased prime factor of a random integer](../RESULTS.md#size_biased_prime_factor_frobenius_class_expansion) (Theorem 24.5, page 451; proved): The probability that a size biased prime factor of a uniform random integer below x has Frobenius in a given conjugacy class is the Chebotarev density plus an explicit term of order 1/log x built from Euler's constant and the Euler Kronecker constant of the class; this is part (ii) of a theorem whose parts on the largest prime factor (Dawsey) and on distinct prime factors (Mertens's theorem per class, after Bardestani and Freiberg) are known. New in this book.
- [Asymptotic independence of the Frobenius class and the share of a size biased prime factor](../RESULTS.md#independence_of_class_and_share_of_a_size_biased_prime_factor) (Theorem 24.6, page 454; proved): The share of a size biased prime factor of a random integer is asymptotically uniform, as Donnelly and Grimmett showed, and asymptotically independent of its Frobenius class, which follows the Chebotarev densities, both for uniform integers below x and for the zeta random integer. New in this book.
- [Empty Chebotarev classes and a lower bound for the rate of the Dirichlet process limit](../RESULTS.md#empty_chebotarev_class_and_lower_bound_for_the_rate) (Theorem 24.8, page 457; proved): For the zeta random integer with parameter close to one, a Frobenius class is empty with probability of exact order a power of the distance to one, the exponent being the Chebotarev density of the class and the constant an explicit classical one, and this atom at zero bounds from below the Kolmogorov distance between the share of the class and its Beta limit; these are parts (i) and (ii) of a theorem whose part (iii) is a case of Serre's theorem on frobenian sets. New in this book.
- [Ewens parameters of Euler products are pole orders](../RESULTS.md#ewens_parameters_are_pole_orders) (Theorem 24.9, page 459; proved): For a random integer weighted by an Euler product whose logarithm has nonnegative coefficients, given at the primes by a continuous function of equidistributed labels, the normalised labelled factorisation converges to a Dirichlet process with the weighted equidistribution measure as base measure, and the Poisson Dirichlet parameter is the exponent of the singularity at s equal to one, the order of the pole when there is one; the unlabelled Poisson Dirichlet limits for weighted integers below x are due to Elboim and Gorodetsky (2024), and for arithmetic semigroups to Arratia, Kochman and Miller (2014). New in this book.
- [Upper bound for the rate of the Dirichlet process limit of Chebotarev shares](../RESULTS.md#upper_bound_for_the_rate_of_chebotarev_shares) (Open Problem 24.1, page 462; open): Asks for an upper bound matching the lower bound for the Kolmogorov distance between the share of a Frobenius class in a zeta random integer and its Beta limit, and for a Wasserstein bound on the share vector at the rate at which the means converge. New in this book.
- [Signs of the Mertens deficits of the identity class in symmetric group fields](../RESULTS.md#signs_of_the_mertens_deficits) (Open Problem 24.2, page 462; open): Asks how the sign of the deficit of the identity Frobenius class among the distinct prime factors of random integers is distributed over number fields with symmetric Galois group ordered by discriminant, and along families of trinomials. New in this book.
- [An arithmetic operation realising the product closure of generalized gamma convolutions](../RESULTS.md#an_arithmetic_product_for_frobenius_functionals) (Open Problem 24.3, page 462; open): Asks which operation on representations or L functions, if any, realises Bondesson's closure of generalized gamma convolutions under products of independent variables for the limits of Frobenius functionals. New in this book.
