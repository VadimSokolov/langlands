What do Calegari's theorems on even Galois representations, torsion in cohomology and potential modularity give back in the dictionary of Thorin measures, heat traces and Frobenius equidistribution? Calegari's theorem that, under hypotheses, a two dimensional representation with distinct Hodge Tate weights is odd and modular is read in the Galois heat trace: for weights zero and w the holomorphic and the even archimedean gamma factors differ by an explicit completely monotone function, so that under the generalized Riemann hypothesis only the holomorphic factor gives a completely monotone heat trace for an odd representation, in every weight. Potential automorphy over CM fields makes the Dirichlet process of random ideals with Sato Tate base measure a theorem over every CM field and makes local Thorin positivity unconditional for weight zero forms over such fields. Potential modularity of abelian surfaces makes the Thorin criterion for the Riemann hypothesis well posed for abelian surfaces over the rationals, with numerical tests on a curve of conductor 249. The torsion growth of Bergeron and Venkatesh is compared in shape with the tree entropy theorem for isogeny graphs, and identifying the constants is left open. It proves no Riemann hypothesis and no new case of reciprocity.

## From the literature

- Even Galois representations with distinct Hodge Tate weights are odd and modular under hypotheses (Calegari 2011 and 2012; Fontaine and Mazur 1995); even representations with large image that are not de Rham (Ramakrishna 2002).
- Modularity lifting beyond the Taylor Wiles method (Calegari and Geraghty 2018).
- Potential modularity of elliptic curves over CM fields, the Sato Tate conjecture over CM fields and the Ramanujan conjecture for weight zero forms over CM fields (Allen, Calegari, Caraiani, Gee, Helm, Le Hung, Newton, Scholze, Taylor and Thorne 2023); Sato Tate over totally real fields (Barnet Lamb, Geraghty, Harris and Taylor 2011).
- Potential modularity of abelian surfaces over totally real fields (Boxer, Calegari, Gee and Pilloni 2021).
- Growth of torsion homology of arithmetic groups (Bergeron and Venkatesh 2013) and the torsion Jacquet Langlands correspondence (Calegari and Venkatesh 2019).

## New results

The catalogued results are the parity theorem, the Dirichlet process over CM fields, local Thorin positivity for Bianchi forms, the Thorin criterion for abelian surfaces and four open problems.

- [Parity from positivity of the Galois heat trace in every Hodge Tate weight](../RESULTS.md#parity_from_positivity_in_every_weight) (Theorem 28.4, page 523; proved): The Galois heat traces built with the holomorphic and with the even archimedean gamma factor of Hodge Tate weights zero and w differ, unconditionally, by an explicit completely monotone function, one quarter of the expectation of e^(-x C_w squared) for C_w the hyperbolic secant variable plus an independent Cauchy variable of scale w over 2, so that for an odd representation with entire L function and root number one, under the generalized Riemann hypothesis, the heat trace is completely monotone with the holomorphic factor and not with the even one. New in this book.
- [The Dirichlet process with Sato Tate base measure over a CM field](../RESULTS.md#dirichlet_process_with_sato_tate_base_measure_over_cm_fields) (Theorem 28.5, page 526; proved): For an elliptic curve without complex multiplication over a CM field, the labelled prime factorisation of the random ideal with zeta weights converges to a Gamma process with Sato Tate label law and the normalised factorisation to the Dirichlet process with Sato Tate base measure, and every Frobenius functional converges to a generalized gamma convolution, now a theorem because Sato Tate and the nonvanishing of the twisted symmetric power L functions are known over CM fields. New in this book.
- [Unconditional local Thorin positivity for weight zero forms over CM fields](../RESULTS.md#local_thorin_positivity_for_bianchi_forms) (Corollary 28.6, page 528; proved): For a weight zero cuspidal automorphic representation of GL2 over a CM field, each unramified local L factor with Hecke eigenvalue different from 2, suitably normalised and evaluated at the square root of the Laplace variable, is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution with an explicit comb of Thorin atoms, unconditionally, because the Ramanujan bound at unramified places is now a theorem over CM fields. New in this book.
- [Thorin criterion for the generalized Riemann hypothesis of an abelian surface](../RESULTS.md#thorin_criterion_for_abelian_surfaces) (Theorem 28.7, page 528; proved): For an abelian surface over the rationals with geometric endomorphism ring the integers whose completed L function is entire and does not vanish at the centre (assumed in the statement; entire for the modular surfaces, of which Boxer, Calegari, Gee and Pilloni prove there are infinitely many), the generalized Riemann hypothesis holds exactly when the completed L function at the centre divided by its value at the square root of the Laplace variable is the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution with Thorin atoms at the squared zero ordinates, and a shifted representation holds with no assumption on the zeros. New in this book.
- [Parity of the Galois heat trace without the generalized Riemann hypothesis](../RESULTS.md#parity_without_grh_open_problem) (Open Problem 28.1, page 533; open): Prove without the generalized Riemann hypothesis that for a self dual Galois representation with distinct Hodge Tate weights at most one of the heat traces built with the holomorphic and the even gamma factor is completely monotone. New in this book.
- [The Euler Kronecker drift of Frobenius functionals over a CM field](../RESULTS.md#euler_kronecker_drift_over_a_cm_field_open_problem) (Open Problem 28.2, page 533; open): Determine the Euler Kronecker drift of the Rankin Selberg subordinator of an elliptic curve without complex multiplication over a CM field in terms of the Euler Kronecker constant of the field and a logarithmic derivative of a symmetric square L function, its dependence on the degree of the field, and whether the deficits of the Sato Tate classes can change sign with the field. New in this book.
- [Sato Tate for abelian surfaces as a Dirichlet process](../RESULTS.md#sato_tate_for_abelian_surfaces_as_a_dirichlet_process_open_problem) (Open Problem 28.3, page 533; open): State the Sato Tate conjecture for a generic abelian surface over the rationals as a Dirichlet process with the trace law of the unitary symplectic group of degree four as base measure, and determine which Frobenius functionals have Gamma limits provable from potential modularity of abelian surfaces alone. New in this book.
- [Torsion growth entropy and spanning tree entropy as one statement](../RESULTS.md#torsion_entropy_and_tree_entropy_open_problem) (Open Problem 28.4, page 533; open): Identify the Bergeron Venkatesh constant one over six pi as an entropy of a random walk or Brownian motion on hyperbolic three space, in the sense in which the spanning tree entropy of a regular tree governs the Jacobians of modular curves, and find the common statement of which the two are the p adic and the archimedean cases. New in this book.
