# Chapter 21. Hitting Times and Reciprocity

How do hitting times express reciprocity laws? The chapter reads Kemeny's constant, spanning trees and resistances of reversible chains, hitting rates of prime factors, explosion of pure birth processes and Brownian exit as arithmetic identities. For the supersingular ℓ isogeny walk in characteristic N, Kemeny's constant is a harmonic sum of the factors of the order of J_0(N)(F_ℓ), point counts of elliptic curves of conductor N when all newforms are rational; Hasse Weil bounds follow, and for N congruent to 1 modulo 12 the Kirchhoff index and an isomorphism criterion, for most primes r, between the r parts of the critical (sandpile) group and of J_0(N)(F_ℓ); the asymptotic class number of Tsfasman and Vlăduţ is the spanning tree entropy of the (ℓ+1) regular tree. Chebotarev densities are hitting rates of the largest prime factors of a zeta distributed random integer; the Thorin converse theorem becomes a statement on explosion times, with GRH in its hypothesis; and the functional equation of every odd primitive Dirichlet L function is the identity between the image and eigenfunction expansions of a signed Brownian exit law. No new case of reciprocity, of Artin's conjecture or of GRH is proved.

## From the literature

- Hashimoto's spanning tree formula for isogeny graphs (Hashimoto 1989; Li 2019), read as reciprocity, with the laws of the critical groups as ℓ varies (Munier and Shnidman 2023).
- Brandt matrices (Eichler 1954; Gross 1987), the Eichler Shimura congruence relation (Shimura 1971), and Ogg's conjecture (Ogg 1975), proved by Mazur (1977).
- The asymptotic class number of X_0(N) (Tsfasman and Vlăduţ 2002), spanning trees of regular graphs (McKay 1983), tree entropy (Lyons 2005), vertical Sato Tate (Serre 1997).
- The Kirchhoff index (Gutman and Mohar 1996; Zhu, Klein and Lukovits 1996) and the commute time identity (Chandra, Raghavan, Ruzzo, Smolensky and Tiwari 1996).
- The largest prime factor of a zeta distributed random integer (Lloyd 1984).
- Brownian exit and the functional equation of Dirichlet's beta function (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001, after Ciesielski and Taylor 1962); theta identities from heat kernels (Salminen and Vignat 2024).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Two prime Kemeny formula for supersingular isogeny walks](../RESULTS.md#two_prime_kemeny_formula_for_supersingular_isogeny_walks) (Theorem 21.1, page 377; proved): Kemeny's constant of the random walk along isogenies of prime degree ℓ between the supersingular elliptic curves in characteristic N is a harmonic sum of the factors of the number of points of J_0(N) over the field with ℓ elements, and, when every newform of level N is rational, ℓ+1 times the sum of the reciprocal point counts modulo ℓ of the isogeny classes of elliptic curves of conductor N. New in this book.
- [Isomorphism criterion for sandpile groups of supersingular isogeny graphs and the modular Jacobian](../RESULTS.md#sandpile_group_and_modular_jacobian_isomorphism_criterion) (Proposition 21.5, page 379; proved): For a prime N congruent to 1 modulo 12 and primes r outside a finite set, the r part of the critical (sandpile) group of the supersingular ℓ isogeny graph in characteristic N, which Munier and Shnidman identify with a quotient of the Hecke algebra, is isomorphic to the r part of the group of points of J_0(N) over the field with ℓ elements exactly when Frobenius at ℓ acts nontrivially on each torsion module of J_0(N) cut out by a maximal ideal of the Hecke algebra above r; the criterion is new. New in this book.
- [Resistances and commute times of supersingular isogeny graphs](../RESULTS.md#kirchhoff_index_and_commute_times_of_supersingular_isogeny_graphs) (Theorem 21.6, page 381; proved): For a prime N congruent to 1 modulo 12, the sum of all effective resistances (the Kirchhoff index) of the supersingular ℓ isogeny graph in characteristic N and the mean commute time between its vertices are explicit multiples of its Kemeny constant, a harmonic sum of the factors of the number of points of J_0(N) over the field with ℓ elements. New in this book.
- [Hasse Weil bounds for Kemeny constants and spanning tree counts of isogeny graphs](../RESULTS.md#hasse_weil_bounds_for_kemeny_constants_and_spanning_trees) (Corollary 21.7, page 381; proved): The Weil bound on Hecke eigenvalues becomes two sided bounds, linear in the genus, on the Kemeny constant of every supersingular isogeny walk of prime level and, for prime levels congruent to 1 modulo 12, two sided bounds, exponential in the genus, on the number of spanning trees of the isogeny graph. New in this book.
- [Tree entropy of the regular tree as the asymptotic class number of J_0(N) over a finite field](../RESULTS.md#tree_entropy_as_asymptotic_class_number_of_modular_jacobians) (Theorem 21.8, page 382; proved): As the prime level N grows, the exponential growth rate per unit of genus of the number of points of J_0(N) over the field with ℓ elements, an asymptotic class number given by a theorem of Tsfasman and Vlăduţ, is the spanning tree entropy of the (ℓ+1) regular tree, which therefore also governs the spanning tree counts of the supersingular isogeny graphs of levels congruent to 1 modulo 12; the reading as a tree entropy is, as far as the authors know, new. New in this book.
- [Chebotarev densities as hitting rates of the largest prime factors of a random integer](../RESULTS.md#chebotarev_densities_as_hitting_rates_of_largest_prime_factors) (Theorem 21.10, page 385; proved): For a zeta distributed random integer whose prime factors carry equidistributed labels such as Frobenius classes, in the limit as the zeta parameter tends to one, the largest prime factor with label in a given set arrives, on the scale of the exponential integral, after an exponential time whose rate is the measure of that set, so that reciprocity laws say that a discrepancy set is hit at rate zero; for a single label this is Lloyd's limit law, and the labelled form is new. New in this book.
- [Explosion time criterion for zeros on the critical line](../RESULTS.md#explosion_time_criterion_for_zeros_on_the_critical_line) (Theorem 21.11, page 387; proved): A symmetric entire function of order at most one, nonzero at the centre, has all its zeros on the critical line exactly when a normalised reciprocal of its values along a half line is the Laplace transform of the explosion time of a pure birth process, whose birth rates are then the squared ordinates of the zeros; this is an equivalence restating the book's Thorin duality, after Polson (2018), and proves no Riemann hypothesis. New in this book.
- [Thorin converse theorem at level one as an explosion time](../RESULTS.md#thorin_converse_theorem_at_level_one_as_explosion) (Corollary 21.12, page 388; proved): If a normalised reciprocal of the completed L function of a self dual Galois representation of conductor one, along a half line, is the Laplace transform of the explosion time of a pure birth process, then the representation comes from a level one cusp form satisfying the generalized Riemann hypothesis, and conversely, with a Wald couple between the explosion time and the Pólya variable when the Pólya kernel is nonnegative; the hypothesis contains the generalized Riemann hypothesis, so no new case of reciprocity follows. New in this book.
- [A new proof of the functional equation of odd primitive Dirichlet L functions by a signed Brownian exit law](../RESULTS.md#functional_equation_of_odd_dirichlet_l_functions_from_signed_brownian_exit) (Theorem 21.13, page 389; proved): Weighting the starting point of a Brownian motion killed at the ends of an interval by an odd primitive Dirichlet character, the image and eigenfunction expansions of the resulting signed exit law give the classical theta transformation formula and functional equation of the L function, with its Gauss sum root number, extending to every odd primitive character a derivation known for the character modulo 4 (Biane, Pitman and Yor; Salminen and Vignat). New in this book.
- [Limiting law of sandpile groups of supersingular isogeny graphs in the level aspect](../RESULTS.md#limiting_law_of_sandpile_groups_of_isogeny_graphs_in_the_level_aspect) (Open Problem 21.1, page 392; open): Find the limiting law, as the prime level grows, of the r parts of the critical groups of supersingular isogeny graphs, a question raised by Munier and Shnidman, describe the critical groups at the primes that the isomorphism criterion excludes, and extend the comparison with the modular Jacobian to the prime 2 and to other levels. New in this book.
- [Vertex hitting times of isogeny walks and Gross's formula](../RESULTS.md#vertex_hitting_times_of_isogeny_walks_and_gross_formula) (Open Problem 21.2, page 392; open): Find the distribution over discriminants of the combination of central values of quadratic twists that Gross's formula attaches to hitting times between the reductions of curves with complex multiplication, and decide what the hitting time of the support of the Gross divisor computes. New in this book.
