# Appendix A. A Probability Toolkit

The appendix collects the probability used in the book for readers trained in number theory, with short proofs or standard references: weak convergence, Lévy's continuity theorem and the method of moments on a compact interval; Weyl's equidistribution criterion, Haar measure, the Weyl integration formula for SU(2) and the Peter Weyl theorem; the Kolmogorov discrepancy and the Kolmogorov distribution; the Wiener Ikehara and Hardy Littlewood Karamata Tauberian theorems and infinitely divisible laws; Kolmogorov's two and three series theorems, random series with independent phases, Kronecker's theorem and the Gärtner Ellis large deviation principle; Bernstein's theorem on completely monotone functions, generalized gamma convolutions (GGC) and their Thorin measures, and Pólya frequency functions; the extremal types theorem, the Rényi representation and the maximum of scaled exponentials whose distribution function is the product in the Dedekind eta function; reversible Markov chains on graphs, with spectral decomposition, hitting times, Kac's formula, mixing on Ramanujan graphs and a complete proof of the spectral formula for Kemeny's constant; and Kullback Leibler divergence, Bayes factors, the sequential probability ratio test and posterior consistency. A table of notation closes it. The appendix contains no new results.

## From the literature
